2020 Tomato Variety Trial

Trial Information
• Trial was in Columbia County
• Conventional
• Determinate varieties only with a focus on large 
beefsteaks
• Seeded May 7th, transplanted June 12th
• Harvested four times with one additional harvest 
with no data collection
• Harvested weekly from 8/21/20 to 10/2/20
• Grown on black plastic mulch and drip irrigated, 
24” spacing in-row, 60” centers
